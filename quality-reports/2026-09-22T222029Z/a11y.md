## Keyboard path (abridged; full logs in `evidence/a11y/{chromium,firefox}/keyboard-path.log`)

Login: Tab goes to Username, then Password. Enter with a bad password leaves focus on BODY. Enter with the good password lands on /board with focus on BODY. The board tab order is: search, New Bug, Logout, Open, Closed, 4 sort headers, then rows. Firefox adds an unnamed scroll-wrapper stop before the headers.

Create: Enter on New Bug puts focus in Title. In Chromium, Tab 6 goes to BODY and Tab 7 to search, behind the dialog. Shift+Tab 2 from Title reaches a row behind the dialog in both engines. Escape, Close X and Save all leave focus on BODY.

Edit: Enter or Space on a row focuses Title. Escape, or Save, sends focus to BODY.

Delete: Enter on Delete leaves focus on Delete, under the overlay. Two aria-modal dialogs are open at once. Tab goes to Edit's Cancel, then the confirm's Close. Escape sends focus to BODY. Confirm Delete sends focus to BODY.

Other controls:
- Toggle: Space works, but aria-pressed is null on both buttons.
- Sort: aria-sort switches between ascending and descending, and the arrow is aria-hidden.
- Clear search: focus goes to BODY.
- Logout: /login loads with focus on BODY.

## Test-suite note for the lead

In my scoped Chromium run of `tests/accessibility/accessibility.spec.ts`, "Escape closes the create modal" **failed** (5 passed, 1 failed). The cause is a strict-mode violation: another agent's row titled `[functional] New Bug ...` is exposed as `button` (a11y-03), so `getByRole('button', { name: 'New Bug' })` matches 2 elements. The product is working; the test breaks on shared data. The page-object locator needs `exact: true`. I did not edit tests/.
